For informational purposes only. Not financial advice. This tool does not predict direction. In testing, no model beat "tomorrow = today" for the price itself. It forecasts how far the price is likely to move, using a per-stock HAR volatility model. In a one-time test on 474 S&P 500 stocks it was not used to build, the 80% ranges held the actual close 81–82% of the time at 1, 5, and 20 days.
